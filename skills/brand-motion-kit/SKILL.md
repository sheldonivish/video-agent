## Format
- 9:16 reels, 1080x1920, on a black background.
- Keep safe margins on every side so curved phone corners never cut content (TODO: confirm exact px; past renders keep ~60-80 px side margins).
- 16:9 sources: B-roll layout, content on top and face below. 9:16 sources: full screen.
- Open on B-roll, not on the face.
- Real clips and screenshots are shown in full, never cropped.

## Palette
- Background: #000000 (black, matches the logo).
- Primary / text: #FFFFFF (white, matches the logo).
- Verified tick: #1D9BF0 (X blue, used for the tick next to "beinginvested").

## Motion personality
- Fast, clean, real: real product clips and screenshots carry the video.
- Background sound and transitions kept very low.
- Default motion: damped spring, small overshoot, quick settle.
- Never: cropping real footage, captions over the face, loud whooshes/SFX. TODO: other banned effects not specified.
