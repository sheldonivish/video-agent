# Brand Motion Kit

Read this before any motion or graphic work for a brand below, then read the full spec at `/home/box/agent-data/brand/brand-motion-kit-full.md` (the matching brand section in full).

## How to apply a kit
1. Identify the brand. Never mix marks, colours or type from two brands, except the narrow ivish "by BeingInvested" credit below.
2. Use only the supplied logo files. Never redraw, retype, recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects to a logo. Symbol, wordmark and icon are supplied images, never typed.
3. Don't upscale a logo past its native size; if the shot needs it larger, ask for higher-res masters.
4. Every fact, number and quote is checked against real sources before it goes on screen. Never invent them.
5. Items marked Suggestion in the full spec are inferred; stated rules win.
6. Where the owner's brief and the brand PDF disagree, follow the owner's brief and keep the PDF rule on record.
7. Covers (both brands): 9:16 canvas with the design as a centred 1:1 square and gaps above and below, in the featured brand's vibe, with no BeingInvested logo.

## BeingInvested
- Source: Brand Guidelines, proposed brand system v2, Nov 2026. Symbol and wordmark are current; the rest is proposed.
- Line: "Be in. Stay invested." Tone: Clear, Sourced, Calm, Bold, Direct. One idea per frame.
- Claim labels: SOURCED, REPORTED, OPINION, ILLUSTRATIVE, SPONSORED, UNCONFIRMED. Source line: `Source: [Publication], [date] — link in caption`.
- Colours: Yellow `#FDCB1E` (one yellow per frame), Black `#000000` (ground), Off-white `#F5F5F5` (B, wordmark, headlines), Logo Black `#141414`, Graphite `#1A1A1A`, Smoke `#8A8A8A`, Editorial Orange `#F7931A` (limited, never replaces the yellow i), `#1D9BF0` for the verified tick only. Never off-white on yellow.
- Type: Inter Tight 800, tracking -3% for headlines; Inter 400/600/700 for body and captions; JetBrains Mono 500 for data only. Max two families per layout.
- Marks: the B mark (B standing on a sideways i) plus the BEINGiNVESTED wordmark. The i in the wordmark is a letter, not the ivish icon.
- Wordmark: clear space 1H (H = cap height), min width 120 px / 30 mm. Symbol: clear space 2x on every side, min 32 px tall. Lockups (proposed): gap 2x, symbol about 4x wordmark cap height.
- Logo files in `/home/box/agent-data/brand/beinginvested/logos/`:
  - Symbols (472x591): `symbol-offwhiteB-yellowi.png` (primary, dark grounds), `symbol-blackB-yellowi.png` (primary, light grounds), `symbol-yellowB-offwhitei.png` (alternate on black), `symbol-yellow-mono.png`, `symbol-offwhite-mono.png`, `symbol-blackB-offwhitei.png` (mid-tones only).
  - Wordmarks (740x91): `wordmark-primary-dark.png` (on black), `wordmark-primary-light.png` (on white), `wordmark-limited.png` (mid-tones only).
- Gap: these files are low-res; large use on 1080x1920 needs SVG or 2-3x masters.

## ivish studios (ivish.studio)
- Source: proposed brand system v1, Nov 2026. Line: "Ideas, in motion." Tone: considered, cinematic, clear.
- Icon (approved): upright rounded i with a yellow dot `#FCCB1F`; stem white on dark, Ink `#0A0A0A` on light. Never redraw, recolour, rotate or lay it down (the sideways i is BeingInvested's). Clear space 2D; min 24 px / 8 mm glyph height.
- Icon files in `/home/box/agent-data/brand/ivish/logos/`: `ivish-studios-i-on-black.png` (1000, primary), `ivish-studios-i-on-white.png` (1000), `ivish-studios-i-black-2000.png`, `ivish-studios-i-transparent-2000.png` (over darkened imagery or Graphite).
- Name: "ivish studios", always lowercase, set live in Inter Tight ExtraBold (-3%). Horizontal, inline and stacked lockups are proposed.
- Type: Inter Tight 800 titles; Instrument Serif Italic for one accent phrase per layout; Inter 400/600 body, 600 caps credits.
- Palette: Studio Yellow `#FCCB1F`, Black `#000000`, White `#FFFFFF`, Ink `#0A0A0A`, Graphite `#1A1A1A`, Smoke `#8A8A8A`, Paper `#F4F2EC` (proposed). No orange or cyan.
- Motion: pure black, 2-4 words per beat, ease-out, nothing bounces, one serif accent; end card has the icon centred and flat with the name below. 9:16 safe area y 240-1680.
- Handles share the same icon: Hindi content goes to `@ivish.studio`, English to `@ivishtrades`. End cards say "Follow @ivishtrades" (English) or "Follow @ivish.studio" (Hindi).
- Optional small "by BeingInvested" credit on end cards, using a BeingInvested logo file, always smaller than its native size. Never on covers and never as an equal-size co-brand. Otherwise keep the B and BeingInvested wordmark out of ivish work.
