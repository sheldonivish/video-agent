# Creator case study short

1. **Verify**: open the creator's real profile or channel and record the exact handle, the video or post counts and views with a timestamp. Correct any viral claim (such as '5M views on the first 4 videos') to what the data actually shows.
2. **Script** (video-script-package): hook with the result, who they are, the format or trick, proof (numbers with source tags), the takeaway, and a CTA (comment a keyword to get the prompt or guide). Use the owner's wording word for word once it's approved. **Gate: script approval.**
3. **Visuals**: a screen-recorded or keyframed walkthrough of the real profile (shown in full), real examples of their posts, logos centred in brand colours, and a colour grade that fits the topic. Credit any borrowed characters or art.
4. **VO and mix**: the owner's cloned voice flow; soft whooshes, smooth transitions, music 20-22 dB under the VO. Motion from frame 1, at least 85% moving frames.
5. **QA and package**: frame-review-loop; a cover (9:16 with the content in the middle square) and a YouTube thumbnail; captions per platform; route it to the right accounts by topic. **Gate: post draft approval.**
