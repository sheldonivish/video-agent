# Trending topic scout

Goal: pick a topic that is trending *now* and fits the channel's niche, backed by live data, not guesses.

## Steps
1. **Read the brief.** Note the niche, excluded topics (e.g. war), format (long-form 16:9 vs Shorts) and the target region(s).
2. **vidIQ (if connected).** Load the vidIQ trend-radar, video-ideas and youtube-seo skills. Pull:
   - breakout and outlier videos in the niche and adjacent niches (last 7-30 days), with views vs channel average;
   - trend urgency, durability and saturation;
   - search volume and competition for 3-5 candidate keywords.
3. **Google Trends.** With pytrends (or the web), check each candidate in US, IN and worldwide over the last 7 days and 90 days. Record whether it is rising, peaking or fading.
4. **Social signal.** Use X search or news for the last 48h, plus YouTube search (TubeAlfred or vidIQ) for how many recent videos already cover it.
5. **Score each candidate** (1-5) on: trend momentum, niche fit, story potential (people plus money plus a twist), saturation (lower is better), how long it lasts, and visual sourcing (real screenshots or footage available).
6. **Recommend** the top 1-3 picks, each with a one-line angle, 2-3 title directions (youtube-banger-titles), the evidence numbers with sources and dates, and the risk (e.g. figures still moving).

## Rules
- Every number cites the tool or URL and the time it was pulled. Never invent trend values.
- Flag fast-moving stories whose numbers will change before publish.
- Present the picks to the owner. Don't start scripting until they choose.
