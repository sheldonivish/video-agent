# X post to vertical clip

1. **Fetch**: pull the post with the X tools (text, author, time, media). Download the video with yt-dlp; if there's no video, use the images as an uncropped slideshow (about 5-6s each).
2. **Layout** (1080x1920, black): the page's avatar, display name, @handle and badge in one row; a hook of at most 2 lines above the media (6-12 words, factual, no emoji, one emphasis colour); the media as big as possible, never cropped (letterbox or pillarbox instead); a small credit under the media with the source's name only. Keep equal side margins.
3. **Length**: under 60s. Cut to the strongest segments and keep the source audio. Don't add captions unless asked.
4. **Facts**: the hook and caption only state what the post or media shows. Mark the author's claims as theirs and say figures are as shown on screen and not verified.
5. **Deliver**: the mp4, the chosen hook plus 2 alternatives, any prompt quoted in the post (word for word), and a spaced caption with the credit and 5 hashtags. Don't post; the owner posts.
